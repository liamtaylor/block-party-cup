Block Party Cup

Happy 9th Birthday!

Six games: soccer, water polo, spleef, indoor surfing, penalties and a night-time mob hunt. 2 to 4 players on one screen.

Or play one game on its own:

How the cup works
  • The cup plays all six games in order: Block Soccer, Water Polo, Spleef, Surf Showdown, Penalty Shootout, then Mob Hunt.
  • Each player has a goal in their own colour. Defend yours and score in everyone else's.
  • Winning a round earns 3 cup points, second earns 2, third earns 1.
  • Most cup points after all six games lifts the cup.
  • Game controllers (Xbox too): press a button on each one, then choose "Use controllers". Move through menus with the D-pad, press A to choose, and press Start to begin or pause.
  • On a keyboard, press Esc to pause and M to turn sound on or off.
